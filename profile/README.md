## Built for your centre's everyday work

LessonBell helps education centres manage enrolments, classes and attendance. Our desktop tools connect reception desks with your centre's LessonBell account, keeping everyday attendance straightforward.

## Windows Attendance Assistant

**Scan a QR code. See the result. Get on with your day.**

A small Windows app for reception desks. Connect a supported USB scanner, select your branch and record attendance in LessonBell.

| Simple to start | Clear at the desk | Easy to keep using |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extract the portable ZIP and open the app. | See attendance results with distinct success and failure sounds. | Minimise to the system tray and keep your desk clear. |

**[Explore the app and setup guide →](https://github.com/lessonbell/windows-attendance-releases)**

> **Public release coming soon.** The download page is ready; the first public app package has not been published yet.
